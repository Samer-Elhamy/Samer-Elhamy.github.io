Welcome to Angelo

Shisha Lounge Angelo (also known as Eetcafe Angelo) is Amsterdam's premier hookah lounge on Albert Cuypstraat. Guests visit from across the Netherlands for meticulously prepared shisha, fresh mint tea, craft cocktails, and an intimate lounge atmosphere.

We use 100% natural coconut charcoal and commercial-grade HVAC air turnover ventilation to ensure pristine air quality and comfortable seating.
