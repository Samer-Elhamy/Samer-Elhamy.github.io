Amsterdam's Premier Hookah Bar

Welcome to Shisha Lounge Angelo — the best hookah bar in Amsterdam. Situated in the lively De Pijp neighbourhood, our hookah lounge combines premium hookah flavours with authentic Arabic hospitality in a relaxed, stylish setting.

Known locally as the top hookah lounge Amsterdam has to offer, Angelo welcomes locals, expats, and tourists alike. Whether you prefer classic tobacco flavours or our signature house blends, you'll find your perfect hookah experience here.

What Makes Angelo the Best Hookah Bar in Amsterdam?

Amsterdam has many cafés and bars, but few offer the dedicated hookah experience that Angelo provides. Here's why we stand out:

  • Expert hookah preparation — our staff are trained hookah specialists
  • Premium coal & shisha quality — only the best equipment for a clean, smooth smoke
  • Comfortable seating — lounge sofas and traditional setups for groups of 2–10+
  • Full drinks menu — from Arabic teas to premium cocktails (€4–€20)
  • Group bookings — perfect for birthday parties, company outings, and social gatherings
  • No hidden fees — transparent pricing, what you see is what you pay

Hookah Amsterdam: Frequently Asked

Is hookah the same as shisha? Yes — hookah, shisha, nargileh, waterpijp, chicha, and nargile all refer to the water pipe smoking experience. At Angelo, we use all these terms interchangeably and welcome guests regardless of which word they use.

Is hookah allowed indoors in Amsterdam? Angelo operates in compliance with Dutch regulations. Ask our staff about current indoor/outdoor setup options.
